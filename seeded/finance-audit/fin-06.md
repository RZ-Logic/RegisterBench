To the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors:

In this memorandum, we'll walk through management's assessment of internal control over financial reporting as of November 1, 2020. Management is responsible for establishing and maintaining adequate internal control over financial reporting, and the assessment was performed based on criteria established in the Internal Control - Integrated Framework (2013) issued by the Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission. Internal control over financial reporting is designed to provide reasonable assurance regarding the reliability of financial reporting and the preparation of financial statements in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles. Based on that assessment, management concluded that no material weakness existed as of the fiscal year end.

So what changed this year? Quite a bit. The control environment absorbed a fiscal year conducted substantially under remote working conditions. Key reconciliation, review, and certification controls operated on schedule. Tested. Documented. Certified. Where COVID-19 disrupted the operation of a control, management implemented a comprehensive framework of compensating controls, and the external auditor evaluated those compensating controls as part of its integrated audit. The auditor's report, dated December 17, 2020, expressed an unqualified opinion on the Company's internal control over financial reporting, and the related statements of consolidated comprehensive income were covered by the same integrated audit.

That's a great question from the committee, and one management considered carefully: whether remote certification streamlined the close process or merely relocated its risks. Honestly, the evidence supports the former. Cycle times for the close shortened by two days against the prior year, and the number of post-close adjustments declined. As of my last update, no control deficiencies aggregating to a significant deficiency had been identified in the fourth quarter, and none were identified through the date of this memorandum.

In conclusion, management's assessment stands, the external auditor concurs, and the certification schedule for the coming fiscal year has been distributed to control owners. I hope this summary helps clarify our approach. The committee is invited to direct questions to the corporate controller in advance of the February meeting.
