Microsoft is a technology company whose mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. We generate revenue by offering a wide range of cloud-based and other services to people and businesses; licensing and supporting an array of software products; designing, manufacturing, and selling devices; and delivering relevant online advertising to a global audience. Our platforms serve customers from startups to superpowers, and we work to foster an ecosystem in which partners build durable businesses on our infrastructure.

Let's take a closer look at the drivers behind these results. Commercial cloud revenue increased 36% to $51.7 billion. Office Commercial products and cloud services revenue increased 12%, driven by Office 365 Commercial growth of 24%. Server products and cloud services revenue increased 27%, driven by Azure growth of 56%. Search advertising revenue, excluding traffic acquisition costs, was relatively unchanged. Azure serves as a cornerstone of the commercial cloud, and it is widely regarded as the primary engine of the Company's growth.

Our industry is dynamic and highly competitive, with frequent changes in both technologies and business models. Each industry shift is an opportunity to conceive new products in an ever-evolving market. The markets for software, devices, and cloud-based services are dynamic, and our competitors are deploying competing cloud-based services for consumers and businesses. We must continue to evolve and adapt over an extended time in pace with this dynamic environment, supported by a robust balance sheet. The investments we are making in infrastructure and devices will continue to increase our operating costs and may decrease our operating margins.

What does this mean for the year ahead? Make no mistake: aggregate demand for our software, services, and devices remains correlated to global macroeconomic and geopolitical factors. Our international operations provide a significant portion of total revenue and expenses, many denominated in currencies other than the U.S. dollar, so changes in foreign exchange rates may affect reported results. Strengthening of the U.S. dollar relative to certain foreign currencies reduced reported revenue and expenses from our international operations in fiscal year 2020.

The result: a durable franchise. Management believes the breadth of the commercial cloud, the scale of the installed base, and the depth of the partner channel position the Company to sustain growth across economic cycles.
